\section{Puzzle generation}\label{app:puzzles}
% TODO: the generator and judge prompts, and every check.

The models solve these puzzles by reading the board, not by remembering them. An earlier experiment on 2026-11-01 tested this. Sol, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Haiku each made one bonus solve on 100 aired bonus puzzles and on 100 generated ones that no model can have seen. Each puzzle was tried twice: once from a blank board showing only the category and word lengths, and once with R S T L N E alone. The generated deck matched aired puzzles in its share of stock phrases, 12\%, since a stock phrase can be guessed from its word lengths.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
 & \multicolumn{2}{c}{Blank board} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{R S T L N E only} & Gap with R S T L N E \\
 & aired & generated & aired & generated & (95\%) \\
\midrule
Sol & 5\% & 3\% & 89\% & 90\% & $-1$ ($-9$ to $+7$) \\
DeepSeek & 4\% & 5\% & 72\% & 67\% & $+5$ ($-8$ to $+18$) \\
Haiku & 0\% & 0\% & 22\% & 30\% & $-8$ ($-20$ to $+4$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

No model recalled aired answers from a blank board. With R S T L N E showing, aired and generated puzzles scored alike, with every gap's interval spanning zero.

\section{Categories}\label{app:categories}

The deck uses 35 categories in the main rounds and 16 in the bonus round. Each category's share follows aired puzzles from 2013 to 2025, and those 35 cover 90\% of aired main puzzles. The players see only the category's name, as contestants do. The descriptions below are what the generator was asked to write, word for word. The judge checks every answer against its category.

Five categories have a rule that code checks, marked $\dagger$. Same Letter needs every word to start with the same letter. Rhyme Time needs two main words that rhyme in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. What Are You Doing? needs a word ending in ING. For Before \& After and Same Name, the judge names the two phrases, and code checks that they rebuild the answer. Categories marked $\ddagger$ must be real. The generator names each answer's source, and the answer must pass the judge twice.
